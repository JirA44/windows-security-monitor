# Official reporting routes

The France routing profile is based on these official sources, checked 2026-11-08:

- [CERT-FR contact and scope](https://cert.cyber.gouv.fr/contact/) - incident declaration routes, published email and telephone, scope of technical response, and explicit distinction from police/judicial complaint services.
- [CERT-FR territorial CSIRTs](https://cert.cyber.gouv.fr/csirt/csirt-territoriaux/) - local assistance network for eligible organizations.
- [17Cyber](https://17cyber.gouv.fr/) - diagnosis and orientation for cybercrime victims.
- [CNIL personal-data breach notification service](https://www.cnil.fr/fr/services-en-ligne/notifier-une-violation-de-donnees-personnelles) - official channel and risk-based controller responsibilities.
- [Service-Public: complaint to the procureur de la R�publique](https://www.service-public.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/R11469) - formal complaint route.
- [Cybermalveillance: response for organization leaders](https://www.cybermalveillance.gouv.fr/tous-nos-contenus/bonnes-pratiques/cyberattaque-que-faire-guide-dirigeants) - preserve logs/evidence and coordinate incident response.

Contacts, eligibility, and filing procedures may change. Recheck the official pages before sharing evidence. The tool does not infer legal obligations or submit formal notifications.
